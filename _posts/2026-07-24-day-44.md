what_i_learned: |
  Today began with our symposium walkthrough, where we reviewed the schedule and learned the order in which each research group will present during the final symposium. Seeing the complete agenda made the event feel much more real because I could finally picture how the day will unfold. We also learned about the keynote speakers who will be presenting during the symposium, which made me even more excited because it will be an opportunity to hear from experienced professionals and researchers. In addition, we were reminded that the awards for the elevator pitch videos will be announced on Sunday. Knowing that our team's hard work will be recognized during the awards ceremony has given me something to look forward to. Whether or not we receive an award, I am proud of the creativity, teamwork, and effort that went into developing our elevator pitch and communicating our research in a meaningful way. Today's walkthrough also reminded me that preparing for a research symposium involves much more than creating slides and practicing a presentation. It requires organization, professionalism, teamwork, and attention to detail so that the event runs smoothly for every participant. Watching the schedule come together helped me appreciate the amount of planning required to organize an event that showcases the work of multiple research groups. I also reflected on how much our team has accomplished since the beginning of the internship, from downloading datasets and training models to writing a research paper and preparing a professional presentation. Seeing our project included alongside the other research teams reinforced that every group has contributed meaningful work throughout the summer. Overall, today increased my excitement for the symposium and reminded me that this experience represents the culmination of weeks of dedication, learning, and collaboration.

blockers: |
  Although today was primarily focused on preparation and team-building activities, I still recognized a few personal challenges that I want to continue improving before the symposium. One challenge is managing my excitement while also staying focused on delivering a polished presentation. As the symposium gets closer, I naturally think about wanting everything to go perfectly, but I also know that becoming overly anxious could affect my confidence. I have been reminding myself to trust the preparation that my team and I have put into this project over the past several weeks. Instead of worrying about every small detail, I want to concentrate on communicating our research clearly and confidently. This mindset has helped me feel more prepared and less overwhelmed as the final presentation approaches. Another challenge is continuing to build relationships with fellows outside of my own research group. Throughout the internship, I spent most of my time working closely with my teammates, so I did not always have many opportunities to interact with participants from the other research labs. Field Day helped break that barrier by encouraging everyone to collaborate, compete, and have fun together in a relaxed environment. It reminded me that networking and building friendships are important parts of professional development, not just completing research projects. I want to continue making an effort to connect with more people before the program concludes because these relationships may become valuable throughout graduate school and my future career. Today's activities encouraged me to step outside my comfort zone and engage with others in a different setting.

reflection: |
  One of the highlights of today was participating in Field Day at Thurgood Marshall. After spending so many weeks focused on coding, writing, reading research papers, and preparing presentations, it was refreshing to step away from the laboratory and simply enjoy spending time with everyone in the program. I was on the Black Team, and we had an amazing time competing in the different activities. One of my favorite moments was winning the blindfold race challenge, which required teamwork, communication, and trust among teammates. There was a lot of laughter, friendly competition, and encouragement throughout the afternoon, making the experience both exciting and memorable. Moments like these reminded me that building strong relationships with others is just as important as developing technical research skills. Reflecting on today, I feel grateful for the balance that the SAIRI program has provided throughout the summer. The program has challenged me academically through research while also creating opportunities to build friendships, collaborate with others, and enjoy experiences outside of the lab. I am especially excited for the symposium because it represents the opportunity to share everything we have learned and accomplished during the internship. I am also looking forward to Sunday's elevator pitch awards because every team invested significant time and creativity into producing their videos. Regardless of the final results, I am proud of the work my teammates and I have completed and the growth I have experienced throughout this journey. Overall, today reminded me that meaningful research experiences are strengthened not only by scientific discovery but also by the friendships, teamwork, and memories created along the way.

what_i_learned: |
  Today was the second-to-last day of the SAIRI program, and it gave me an opportunity to slow down and reflect on everything I have accomplished throughout the summer. Most of the day was spent completing the final tasks before returning home, including finishing my daily research blog posts, making the final revisions to our research paper, and organizing my belongings so I would be ready to travel tomorrow. Although these tasks seemed simple compared to training models or preparing presentations, they reminded me that completing a research project requires attention to detail from beginning to end. I also realized how important documentation and organization are because they ensure that all of the work completed during the internship is preserved and can be shared with others. Looking back at everything we created, including our research paper, presentation, poster, elevator pitch video, and blog posts, I feel incredibly proud of how much our team accomplished together. Today helped me appreciate that every small task contributes to the success of a much larger project. As I finished reviewing our research paper one last time, I reflected on how much my understanding of artificial intelligence and healthcare research has grown since the first day of the internship. Before this program, many of the concepts we studied, including deep learning, fairness evaluation, explainability, robustness testing, and statistical validation, were completely new to me. Through weeks of reading scientific literature, coding, writing, presenting, and collaborating with my mentors and teammates, I have developed a much stronger foundation in research. I also gained a greater appreciation for the amount of effort required to produce research that is accurate, reproducible, and meaningful. Beyond the technical knowledge, I learned the importance of teamwork, communication, and accepting constructive feedback throughout the research process. This experience has shown me that research is a continuous learning process, and every challenge presents an opportunity to grow.

blockers: |
  Although today did not include any major research obstacles, one challenge was balancing the excitement of returning home with the realization that this incredible experience is coming to an end. As I packed my belongings and completed my final tasks, I found myself reflecting on how quickly the summer had passed. It felt bittersweet because I was excited to see my family again, but I also knew that I would miss the daily interactions with my mentors, teammates, and fellow researchers. Throughout the program, I became comfortable with the routine of learning, collaborating, and solving problems together. Knowing that tomorrow would be my final day made me appreciate every conversation and every moment even more. Rather than focusing on the ending, I chose to appreciate how much this experience has prepared me for future opportunities. Another challenge was making sure that every final detail was completed before leaving. I wanted to ensure that my blog posts accurately reflected my experiences, that our research paper was polished, and that I had packed everything needed for my trip home. Managing several closing responsibilities at once required good organization and time management. I also wanted to leave knowing that I had given my very best effort throughout the internship and had completed every assignment to the highest standard possible. Taking the time to review everything one last time gave me peace of mind and confidence that I was leaving the program on a strong note. These final responsibilities reminded me that professionalism includes finishing projects with the same level of care and dedication that was shown at the beginning.

reflection: |
  Reflecting on today, I feel an overwhelming sense of gratitude for everything this internship has given me. This summer has been much more than a research experience; it has been an opportunity for personal, academic, and professional growth. I entered the program eager to learn about artificial intelligence, but I am leaving with a much deeper understanding of research, scientific writing, public speaking, collaboration, and problem-solving. I have gained confidence in my ability to contribute to meaningful research and to communicate complex ideas in a way that others can understand. Every mentor, teammate, and fellow participant played a role in helping me become a stronger student and a more confident researcher. Looking back, I can honestly say that this experience has exceeded every expectation I had when I first arrived. As I prepare to leave tomorrow, I know that the lessons I learned during SAIRI will stay with me long after the program ends. This internship has strengthened my desire to continue participating in research because I have seen firsthand how scientific discoveries can improve healthcare and positively impact people's lives. It has also shown me that success comes from remaining curious, asking thoughtful questions, accepting feedback, and never being afraid to challenge myself. I am excited to take everything I have learned back to Xavier University of Louisiana and apply these experiences to my future academic, research, and professional goals. While I am sad that this chapter is coming to a close, I know it is only the beginning of many more research opportunities in the future. Overall, this experience has inspired me to continue growing as a scholar, researcher, and future healthcare professional, and I will always be grateful for everything I learned throughout this unforgettable summer.

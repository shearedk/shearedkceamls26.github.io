what_i_learned: |
  Today marked the final day of the SAIRI Institute, and it was a bittersweet experience as I finished packing and prepared to travel back home. As I reflected on the past several weeks, I realized just how much I have grown both academically and personally. This program introduced me to areas of research that I had never explored before, including deep learning, artificial intelligence in healthcare, fairness evaluation, explainability, and reproducibility. I also gained hands-on experience with Python programming, scientific writing, reading research literature, and presenting technical information to diverse audiences. Before this internship, many of these concepts seemed intimidating, but now I feel much more confident discussing them and understanding how they can be applied to solve real healthcare problems. Looking back, I can honestly say that this experience has given me knowledge and skills that will continue to benefit me throughout graduate school and my future career. Beyond the technical skills I developed, I learned valuable professional lessons that cannot be taught in a classroom alone. Working alongside faculty mentors, graduate mentors, and fellow researchers showed me the importance of collaboration, communication, and accepting constructive feedback throughout the research process. Every coding challenge, manuscript revision, presentation rehearsal, and team discussion helped me become a more patient, thoughtful, and confident researcher. I also learned that research is rarely a straight path and that progress often comes through persistence, continuous learning, and a willingness to revise your work. The relationships I built throughout this program were just as meaningful as the technical knowledge I gained, and I know many of these connections will continue beyond this summer. Most importantly, this internship showed me that I am capable of contributing to meaningful research that has the potential to improve healthcare and positively impact people's lives.

blockers: |
  Although today did not involve any research-related obstacles, one challenge was saying goodbye to a place and a group of people who had become such an important part of my summer. Over the past several weeks, the institute became more than just a research program; it became a community where I was encouraged to learn, ask questions, and grow. Packing my belongings made everything feel real, and I found myself reflecting on how quickly the experience had come to an end. It was difficult knowing that I would no longer see my mentors and fellow researchers every day. At the same time, I recognized that this is a natural part of every meaningful experience and that the lessons and relationships I gained will continue long after I leave. Instead of focusing only on the ending, I chose to appreciate how much this program has changed me for the better. Another challenge was thinking about how to carry everything I have learned into the next stage of my academic journey. This internship exposed me to advanced topics in artificial intelligence, machine learning, healthcare research, and scientific communication, and I now feel a greater responsibility to continue building upon these skills. I know there is still so much more for me to learn, particularly in programming, advanced data analysis, and developing AI models. Rather than viewing these areas as weaknesses, I now see them as exciting opportunities for continued growth. This experience has given me a strong foundation, but it has also shown me that learning never truly ends. I am leaving with both confidence in what I have accomplished and motivation to continue challenging myself in the future.

reflection: |
  Reflecting on this entire journey, I can confidently say that participating in the SAIRI Institute has been one of the most impactful experiences of my undergraduate education. I arrived eager to learn, but I am leaving with a completely new perspective on research, technology, and the role that artificial intelligence can play in improving healthcare. Throughout the program, I contributed to a research paper, presented at a professional symposium, created an award-winning elevator pitch, collaborated with an outstanding research team, and developed skills that I never imagined I would have in such a short amount of time. Every challenge pushed me outside of my comfort zone and helped me become a stronger student, researcher, and future professional. This experience also strengthened my confidence and reminded me that growth happens when I am willing to embrace unfamiliar opportunities. I will always be grateful for everything this program has taught me and for the people who invested their time in helping me succeed. As I return home, I do not see today as the end of my research journey but as the beginning of many new opportunities. This internship has inspired me to continue pursuing research throughout graduate school and beyond because I have discovered how rewarding it is to contribute to work that can make a real difference in people's lives. I hope to continue exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, healthcare, and psychology while building upon the skills I developed this summer. I also hope to return to programs like SAIRI in the future, whether as a researcher, mentor, or collaborator, because I would love the opportunity to give back to future students in the same way others invested in me. As I close this chapter, I leave with lasting friendships, unforgettable memories, valuable professional experiences, and a renewed passion for research and lifelong learning. Overall, this internship has become a defining part of my undergraduate journey, and I know the lessons I learned here will continue to guide me throughout my future career.

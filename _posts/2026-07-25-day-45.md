what_i_learned: |
  Today marked the first day of the NSEA Research Symposium, and it was an inspiring experience from beginning to end. One of the highlights of the day was listening to the keynote speakers, who shared their experiences, research journeys, and advice for students pursuing careers in science, technology, engineering, and healthcare. Their presentations reminded me that every successful researcher started somewhere and that persistence, curiosity, and continuous learning are essential for long-term success. Hearing them speak reinforced the importance of remaining open to new opportunities and embracing challenges that lead to personal and professional growth. Throughout the day, I also had the opportunity to network with faculty members, researchers, and students from different institutions. Every conversation helped me realize how valuable networking can be because it creates opportunities to learn from others, exchange ideas, and build relationships that may benefit future collaborations. Another memorable part of today was walking through the research poster session and seeing the incredible work completed by students from different universities. I was especially excited to see our own research poster professionally printed and displayed among so many other impressive projects. It felt rewarding to see months of hard work transformed into a professional presentation that represented everything our team had accomplished throughout the summer. I enjoyed walking around, reading other posters, and learning about research topics outside of my own area of study because it expanded my understanding of the wide range of scientific questions being investigated. I also had the opportunity to meet students and professionals from Howard University and Johns Hopkins University, and I appreciated hearing about their academic experiences and research interests. These conversations reminded me that research is built on collaboration and that every interaction has the potential to open new doors for learning and future opportunities.

blockers: |
  Although today was filled with exciting experiences, I also recognized some personal challenges as I prepared for tomorrow's presentation. As the symposium continued, I became increasingly aware that I would soon be presenting our research in front of faculty members, researchers, and professionals. While I feel confident in my understanding of our project, I naturally experienced some nervousness about speaking in front of such a knowledgeable audience. I found myself thinking about whether I would remember every important point, answer questions effectively, and present with confidence. However, I reminded myself that my team and I have spent weeks preparing, practicing, and refining our presentation. Instead of allowing nervousness to become overwhelming, I chose to view it as motivation to continue practicing and trusting the work that has prepared me for this moment. Another challenge today was balancing my excitement with the desire to absorb as much information as possible from the symposium. There were so many interesting research posters, keynote presentations, and networking opportunities that it was impossible to experience everything in one day. I wanted to spend more time speaking with researchers, asking questions about their work, and learning about different career paths within science and healthcare. This experience reminded me how broad the research community truly is and how many opportunities exist beyond my current academic experience. Although I could not participate in every discussion, I made an effort to introduce myself to new people and learn from the conversations I had. I hope to continue strengthening my networking skills because building professional relationships is an important part of becoming a successful researcher.

reflection: |
  Reflecting on today, I feel incredibly grateful for the opportunity to participate in the NSEA Research Symposium. The keynote speakers, research presentations, and networking opportunities reminded me why I enjoy research and why I want to continue pursuing opportunities that allow me to contribute to meaningful scientific discoveries. Meeting individuals from institutions such as Howard University and Johns Hopkins University inspired me because it showed me the diverse paths researchers take throughout their careers. Every conversation reinforced the importance of remaining curious, asking thoughtful questions, and taking advantage of opportunities to connect with others. Seeing so many talented students and professionals gathered together reminded me that research is a collaborative effort that grows through shared knowledge and mutual support. Today's experience strengthened my motivation to continue developing as both a researcher and a future healthcare professional. As I prepare for tomorrow's presentation, I feel a mixture of excitement, anticipation, and confidence. I know that presenting our project on predictive stability versus fairness instability in clinical ECG classification represents the culmination of everything my team has worked toward throughout the summer. Although I still feel some nervousness, I believe my preparation and practice will allow me to communicate our research effectively. I am proud of how much I have grown since the beginning of the internship, not only in my technical knowledge but also in my confidence, communication skills, and ability to work as part of a research team. Regardless of how tomorrow unfolds, I know that participating in this symposium has already been a valuable learning experience that will continue to shape my future academic and professional journey. Overall, today reignited my passion for research and reminded me that meaningful opportunities often begin with the willingness to learn, connect, and challenge yourself.
